How to think about the PCC partner landscape

Not all PCC integrations are created equal. Partners range from deep, fully-validated enterprise integrations to emerging BYOT connectors still building toward feature parity. Use this guide to quickly orient yourself before a customer conversation — then verify specifics in the Partner Comparison tab.

Agentforce Contact Center — Native Salesforce
First-party voice — no BYOT required

Agentforce Contact Center (ACC) is Salesforce's own built-in voice solution. When a customer has no existing CCaaS investment and is open to a greenfield deployment, ACC is the cleanest path — WebRTC audio, Omni-Channel routing, Einstein AI, and Omni Supervisor all native, with no integration layer between telephony and CRM.

The PCC partner ecosystem exists for customers who already have a CCaaS contract or specific requirements their current provider handles. ACC removes that dependency entirely. Consider it in discovery whenever a customer asks "do we need a telephony partner at all?" — often the answer is no.

When to position ACC: Greenfield contact center, strong Salesforce platform commitment, customers who want to minimize vendor complexity, or those prioritizing the deepest Agentforce AI integration.

Tier 1 — Enterprise Ready
Full-featured, GA, live customers
Amazon · Genesys · Vonage · Natterbox · Talkdesk · Upland InGenius · Odigo · Bucher+Suter · AMC · NICE · Five9

These partners cover the full breadth of PCC features — foundational controls, real-time transcription, Einstein AI, routing, supervisor tools, and reporting — and are validated with live customers. They represent the safest recommendation for enterprise deals.

Amazon Connect is among the most complete, with native AWS ecosystem depth and strong AI/ML capabilities. Genesys is the dominant enterprise CCaaS player globally; its CX Cloud integration is deep but some features (Unified Routing, Omni Flows) are still roadmap. Five9 is consistently strong across the board with live customer validation as of July 2026. NICE CXOne and Talkdesk are solid across all categories with minor gaps in Supervisor Barge-In and Federated Routing Skills respectively. Bucher+Suter (Cisco-based) is the go-to for existing Cisco CCE or WxCC customers — near-complete coverage with a few roadmap items on transcription and routing.

When to recommend Tier 1: Customer has an existing CCaaS contract, needs proven integration, or is in a regulated industry where risk tolerance is low.

Tier 2 — Strong with Gaps
GA with some features on roadmap
Dialpad · RingCentral (RingEX & RingCX) · AirCall · Twilio · Zoom · Unaric (Mirage) · Enreach · Axialys · Diabolocom · Worldline / Magellan

These partners have solid GA status and live customers, but carry more roadmap items — particularly around Supervisor tooling, advanced routing, and Einstein AI features. They're often strong fits for specific geographies or customer profiles.

Twilio is powerful for technically sophisticated customers who want flexibility (Flex, Voice, or Conversations), but requires more implementation effort. RingCentral has two distinct paths: RingEX (more mature, though several items still roadmap) and RingCX BYOT (targeting full feature parity by end of year). Zoom is an excellent choice for customers already deep in the Zoom ecosystem with strong UCaaS (Teams-equivalent) needs. Enreach (formerly Benemen) and Axialys are strong EMEA-focused options. Diabolocom has solid validation as of August 2026 with broad feature coverage.

When to recommend Tier 2: Customer has a preferred vendor relationship, geographic requirement (EMEA, APAC), or specific use case (e.g. UCaaS-first) that maps well to one of these partners.

Tier 3 — Emerging / Validate Carefully
Beta, limited validation, or sparse data
Google · Sprinklr · NTT Techncross · Comdesign · Cloudtalk

These partners are either still in Beta, have limited last-validated dates, or have sparse feature data in the matrix. That doesn't mean they can't work — it means you should do extra due diligence before positioning them in a customer conversation.

Google is in Beta with strong potential but not yet GA. Sprinklr has no last-validated date and many blank cells — verify directly with the partner team before positioning. NTT Techncross and Comdesign are marked as needing updates. Cloudtalk has incomplete data.

When to recommend Tier 3: Only with direct partner confirmation and a clear customer reason (e.g. existing Sprinklr digital engagement investment, or Google CCAI strategy).

Special Consideration — BYOC / BYO-CCaaS
Bring Your Own Channel or CCaaS
Amazon · Genesys · Vonage · Bucher+Suter (WxCC) · Five9 · Zoom · Enreach · Axialys

BYOC and BYO-CCaaS capabilities allow customers to route non-Salesforce channels through PCC, or bring their existing CCaaS routing engine into the Salesforce UI. This is a key differentiator for customers with complex omnichannel environments.

BYO Channel (routing external channels into Salesforce) is supported by Amazon, Genesys, Vonage, Bucher+Suter WxCC, Five9, Zoom, and Enreach — with others on roadmap. BYO Channel for Agentforce (ASA) is more selective: Genesys is the strongest, with others in various roadmap stages. If a customer is asking about BYOC, Genesys or Amazon are typically the safest starting points.

Key Decision Factors
How to choose in a discovery conversation

Existing CCaaS contract? Start there — look up the partner in the matrix and walk through the gaps. Most Tier 1 partners are strong enough to work with.

Einstein AI is critical? Prioritize partners with full Transcription + Einstein GPT + Conversation Insights support: Amazon, Genesys, Vonage, Natterbox, Talkdesk, Five9, and Twilio.

Supervisor tooling matters? Listen-In and Barge-In are the most commonly missing features. Amazon, Genesys, Vonage, Natterbox, Talkdesk, Five9, Twilio, and Zoom all support both.

Customer is EMEA? Enreach (Nordics), Axialys (France), Odigo (France/EU), and Diabolocom (France/EU) are purpose-built for that market.

Unified Routing is a must? This is the most frequently missing feature. Amazon, Talkdesk, Five9, AMC (Avaya/Cisco), and Diabolocom support it; most others have it on roadmap.
